# 钩子库

钩子 = 视频第一句话,任务是让人停下来。本库按"勾人的机制"分 13 族,**不分品类**:任何一族都可以套在任何商品上,例句注明的原商品只是帮助理解。

## 怎么选

1. **先问:这条视频最硬的东西是什么?**按商品特点选族,不按品类:
   - 效果一眼看得见 → 1 结果先行
   - 观众有明确、自己知道的痛 → 2 痛点共情
   - 商品有反常识的点、大家没见过 → 3 好奇悬念
   - 有落差、有意外,或观众会怀疑(价格高、看着像智商税)→ 4 反差反转
   - 有常见的错误做法或坑 → 5 避坑
   - 价格有冲击,或省钱省事 → 6 价格利益
   - 买家人群很明确 → 7 人群点名
   - 需要建立信任 → 8 真心推荐
   - 家人朋友能自然出场 → 9 身边人证明
   - 有一个带情绪的场景 → 10 故事开场
   - 多用途,或和同类对比 → 11 盘点清单
   - 能让人忍不住回答 → 12 互动提问
   - 有节日、事件、名人、热剧可以借 → 13 借势
2. **一条只选一个主族,最多叠一个辅助族。**三个以上效果会被稀释。
3. **悬念可以留,承诺必须兑现。**开头说了什么,后面一定要给到。
4. **轮换:**同一个对话里的几条换着用不同的族;用户说以前用过哪族,也避开。
5. **句式只借思路**:下面的句式是框架,按人设口语化重写,不照填。

## 怎么用句式

- 句式是骨架,不是成品。`[ ]` 里填真实素材:分析里的痛点、爽点、买家原话,商品图上的数字(我们自己的价格不报数字,见第 6 族)。
- 按口播样本的说话方式写顺,**不整句照搬例句**(和任一例句连续 6 个词相同 = 重写)。
- 库里没有合适的,可以自己写新钩子,在交付的【钩子】栏标 **"库外新写"** 并说明为什么。
- 用户说"换钩子"或"再出 N 个":按族、句式、素材组合出候选,每条一行(族|[短画面]|第一句|中文),每轮不重复,也不和这个对话里已出的重复。
- 用户说"**存入钩子库**"并给出新钩子:按族归类加到对应族末尾,标来源和日期;和已有的重复就不收。
- 价格、销量、涨价、断货、名人说过的话,必须真实(见 platform-2026.md)。

## 开头三拍:顺着说 → 刹车 → 拉回来
来自 Kallaway 的开头写法。不是新的一族,是搭开头的结构,常套在 4 反差反转、5 避坑、2 痛点共情上;用户说"反写"时,每个立场的开头都按它写。
1. **顺着说**:一句观众会点头的话,把话题说清楚(大家都这么做、都这么以为的)。对这个话题没兴趣的人会自己划走,留下来的都是准买家。
2. **刹车**:一个转折:But / Except / Pero。
3. **拉回来**:往反方向说,说出一个没想到、但正文能证明的真相。顺着说得越真、越像观众自己,拉回来越狠。
- **3 秒左右说完**:英语大约顺着说 4-5 个词 + 刹车 + 拉回来 4-5 个词(西语按 3.3 词/秒算)。带货视频没时间慢慢铺,还没听到"但是"人就划走了。
- **画面先抓人**:顺着说那句是平的,第一帧画面要有东西(商品正在用、问题摆在眼前),按 `02-storyboard.md` 第三节。
- 拉回来那句必须兑现,不夸大。
- 压成一句的样子:真实开头 #29 "This is the most boring thing that I've promoted on TikTok Shop, but probably one of my favorite things."

## 1 结果先行
先把最炸的结果甩出来,观众想知道怎么做到的。

- `[N] minutes. That's all it took to [result].` — 就 N 分钟,[结果]就搞定了(时间+结果)
- `From [bad state] to [good state] in [time].` — 从 X 到 Y,只用了多久
- `Here's the before… and here's after [X].` — 这是之前……这是用了之后
- `A $[X] fix for a $[Y] problem.` — 花 X 块解决一个值 Y 块的问题(最低成本+最高回报)
- `I'm not a [pro], and I just [result].` — 我不是专业的,但我刚刚[结果](普通人+不普通结果)
- `Ten seconds. Watch the difference.` — 十秒钟,看区别(最短时间+最大变化)
- `I refused to pay $[X] for [service]. Did this instead.` — 我不肯花 X 块请人,自己这么干了(拒绝+结果)
- `I did not expect this to actually [result].` — 真没想到它真能[结果]
- `Everyone said this wouldn't work. [Result].` — 所有人都说这不行,结果……(被质疑+结果)
- `What happens when I only use this on one side…` — 我只在一半用会怎样(半边对比)
- `Look how [clean / smooth / quiet] this is…` — 看看这有多[干净/顺/安静]
- `POV: you finally found a [X] that actually [works].` — 第一视角:你终于找到一个真管用的 X
- `The way I gasped when [X]…` — [X]的时候我倒吸一口气
- `I tried the laziest way to do this, and it actually worked.` — 我试了最懒的方法,居然也做到了
- `How much [water] can this [towel] actually [absorb]? Watch this.` — 这个毛巾到底能吸多少水?看好了(反向设置实验)
- `Watch what this does.` / `Wait for it…` — 看它干了什么 / 等着看
- `Using [old thing] a hundred times doesn't do what this does once.` — 用旧的一百次不如用它一次
- `If these socks ever stink, I'll eat them.` — 这袜子你要能穿臭,塞我嘴里都行(夸张口语)
- `What did I do to deserve this [X]?` — 我何德何能遇到这个(这是来报恩的吗)

- 西语示例(测试稿,未经数据验证):
  - `Así llegaba a [地方] antes… y así llego ahora.` — 以前我是这样到的……现在是这样
  - `POV: por fin un [X] que no se te baja.` — 第一视角:终于有个不往下掉的 X
  - `¿Aguanta [X]? Vamos a ver.` — 它扛得住吗?试试看

## 2 痛点共情
先戳最疼的地方,让观众觉得"说的就是我"。

- `Anyone else's [X] [do this]?` / `Is it just me, or does [X]…?` — 有没有人跟我一样……(你是不是也这样)
- `I was literally just talking to someone about this today.` + 痛点 — 我刚刚还在和别人聊这个事
- `I hate to break it to you, but…` — 我不想打击你,但是……
- `I never realized how bad [problem] was until I used this.` — 用了这个我才知道[问题]有多严重(刷新认知)
- `I get it. [Pain] is the worst.` — 我懂你,[痛点]真的烦死人
- `Stop [doing X].` — 别再[X]了
- `Why does [X] always [Y], even when you [Z]?` — 为什么明明……却还是……
- `Who else knows the joy of not having to [X] after [Y]?` — 谁能体会每天[Y]完不用[X]的快乐
- `Why do people still use [X] for [Y]? I use this.` — 咋还有人用 X 干 Y,我都用这个
- `If you don't want to end up [bad state], keep one of these.` — 不想变成那样,就备一个这个
- `That smell in your [sink]? That's [what's actually rotting].` — 你水槽那股味,是[真相](后果放大)
- `It's [2026] and you're still [doing X]?` — 都 2026 了,你还在……(最新…不会不知道吧)
- `Are you still using the cheap regular [X]?` — 你还在用普通廉价的 X 吗
  - ES: "¿Sigues usando el [XX] barato de siempre?"

- 西语示例(测试稿,未经数据验证):
  - `¿Por qué traes [A], [B] y [C]… y aun así [痛点]?` — A、B、C 都用上了,怎么还是……
  - `¿Nomás a mí [痛点]?` — 就我一个……吗?

## 3 好奇悬念
利用没见过、不知道的事激发好奇。

- `I was today years old when I found out this exists.` — 我今天才知道世界上有这东西
- `Wait, this doesn't make sense…` — 等等,这说不通啊……
- `After studying thousands of [X], I noticed the good ones all have this in common.` — 我研究了上千个 X,好的 X 都有同一个特点
- `I'd recommend bracing yourself for what I'm about to say…` — 做好心理准备,接下来要说的会吓你一跳(常接"1/10 价格的平替被我挖到了")
- `If I had to start from zero today, I'd only care about this one thing.` — 如果我要从 0 开始,我只会在乎这一件事
- `How to [result] without [pain].` — 怎么不[痛苦]也能[结果]
- `What happens if you [X]?` — 如果你[X]会怎么样
- `Why do [hotels / pros] never have [problem]? This.` — 为什么别人从来没有这个问题?答案是这个
- `99% of people get this wrong — including me.` — 99% 的人都搞错了,包括我
- `You think [X] works like this. It doesn't.` — 你以为 X 是这样的,其实完全不是
- `It took me [N] years to realize you don't need to [X].` — 我花了 N 年才明白根本不用[X]
- `I bet you didn't know [X].` — 我敢打赌你不知道[X]
- `This is the trick most pros will never tell you.` — 这是大多数行家永远不会告诉你的小技巧
- `I promise you've never seen a [X] like this.` — 我保证你绝对没见过这样的 X
- `This isn't your regular [X].` — 这可不是普通的 X
- `No wonder [X] always [Y].` — 怪不得 X 总是 Y
- `This is why [X]'s [Y] is always [Z].` — 这就是 X 的 Y 总是 Z 的秘诀
- `I think this is one of the biggest mistakes [X] make — and they don't even realize it.` — 这是 X 犯的最大错误之一,他们自己都没意识到
- `Here's what nobody tells you about [X].` — 关于 X 没人告诉你的事
- `[N] things about [X] that feel illegal to know.` — 关于 X 的 N 件事,知道了都觉得像犯规
- `A [Japanese / German] [chef] recommended this to me.` / `Every home in [Japan] has one of these.` — 这是日本/德国的[厨师]推荐给我的(国别推荐)

- 西语示例(测试稿,未经数据验证):
  - `¿[A], [B] o [C]? Esta cosa es las tres.` — 是 A、B 还是 C?它三样都是
  - `Cómo [结果] sin [痛苦].` — 怎么不[痛苦]也能[结果]

## 4 反差反转
前后落差、意外、先怀疑再打脸。

- `I thought [X] was a gimmick… until this.` — 我以为 X 是噱头……直到这个(认知反差)
- `This $[5] thing outperformed my $[200] [X].` — 这个 5 块的东西比我 200 块的还好用(极限反差)
- `Unpopular opinion: [X].` — 不受欢迎的观点:[X](观点冲突)
- `Hear me out.` — 先听我说完
- `I tested something that shouldn't have worked…` — 我测了一个本来不该管用的东西……(盲买的平价款,居然比大牌还好用)
- `Don't knock it till you try it.` — 没试过先别急着嫌弃
- `You'd never guess how different these two [X] are.` — 你做梦也想不到这两个差别这么大
- `I thought I got scammed again — turns out it actually [works].` — 我以为又被骗了,没想到真能[用]
- `I almost didn't open it because of the packaging.` — 差点被包装劝退,用完真香
- `Everyone says it looks plain. I just want to know if it's back in stock.` — 别人嫌它朴素,我只想问还有没有货
- `Don't buy this. Seriously. You'll end up rebuying it forever.` — 别买,千万别买,买完你会一直回购
- `Yeah, I'm returning this [X].` → 正话反说塞卖点 → `The real reason? I paid too much. It's [65]% off now.` — 我要退货……真正原因是我买贵了
- `Don't be fooled by how plain it looks.` — 别被它朴素的外表骗了
- `Don't hate me, but [X] changed how I [Y].` / `You're probably going to hate me for saying this…` — 别恨我,但……/ 我说这句话你可能会恨死我
- `You've been using [X] wrong this whole time.` — 你一直都用错了
- `Everything you think about [X] is wrong.` — 你对 X 的认知全错了
- `I bought a cheap $[800] [couch] online.` — 我在网上买了个"便宜的" 800 块沙发(价格认知错位)
- `When I first saw this popping up on TikTok Shop, I was skeptical but intrigued.` — 第一次刷到时我很怀疑,但又好奇
- `You're going to thank me for this.` — 你会回来谢我的(报喜式)
- `[1997] is long gone. [2000] is long gone. Now in [2026]…` — 年代进化对比

- 西语示例(测试稿,未经数据验证):
  - `Mis compas se burlaban de mi [X]… hasta que [时刻].` — 哥们笑我这个 X……直到……
  - `[Precio] por esto… pensé que otra vez me habían visto la cara.` — 才 X 块……我以为又被坑了("me vieron la cara" 是"被坑了"的口语说法)
  - `Me vas a dar las gracias en [mes].` — 到 X 月你会回来谢我的

## 5 避坑
警示、危机感、损失厌恶。

- `Whatever you do, don't [X].` — 千万不要[X]
- `Take your old [X] and get rid of it.` — 把你的旧 X 扔了吧(扔掉老产品,摆脱[坏事])
- `If you keep [X], [bad result].` — 如果你再不……/再这样……
- `Learn from my mistake: [X].` — 我的血泪教训:[X]
- `Don't wait until [bad moment] to get one of these.` — 别等到[那时候]才想起来买
- `If this showed up before you bought one, you just dodged a bad buy.` — 买之前刷到这条,算你少踩一个坑
- `How did I not know about this sooner?!` — 我之前怎么没发现这个
- `Why did nobody tell me this existed sooner?` — 为什么没人早点告诉我
- `Don't buy [X] before you see this.` / `Stop buying [X] before you watch this.` — 买 X 之前先看这个
- `I've used [N] of these. Only now I know how bad the others were.` — 用过几款同类,才知道以前踩坑多离谱
- `Don't wait — they raise the price every year.` — 别等了,每年都涨价(**只在有真实涨价 / 断货依据时用**)

- 西语示例(测试稿,未经数据验证):
  - `¿Por qué nadie me dijo de esto antes de [时刻]?` — 在……之前,怎么没人告诉我这个
  - `Deja [旧东西] pa' [场景]. Te lo digo por experiencia.` — ……别再用旧的了,我吃过亏

## 6 价格利益
价格冲击、省钱、省事。**我们商品自己的价格、原价、折扣百分比不报数字**:句式里 `$[X]` 指我们价格的,换成不带数字的说法(`not even that much`、`less than lunch`、`it's on sale`);别家、商店、修理厂的价格可以报真实数字。

- `Why pay store price for [X] when this exists?` — 为什么要花商店的高价
- `No matter your budget, get one [X].` — 不管预算多少,都买一个
  - ES: "No importa cuál sea tu presupuesto, este producto vale totalmente la pena."
- `I don't even know who priced this — I impulse-bought it instantly.` — 不知道谁定的这个价,我一冲动就买了
- `If I didn't tell you the price, you'd think I spent a fortune.` — 我不说价格,你肯定以为我花了大钱
- `Cost me less than lunch. Feels like a two-hundred-dollar [X].` — 一顿午饭钱,用出几百块的体验
- `I was ready to splurge — and it's only $[X]?` — 都准备咬牙买了,结果就这价?
- `Watch this and save $[X].` — 看完这条省 X 块
- `With this, you'll never have to [X] again.` — 有了它,再也不用[X]
- `I had no idea you could [Y] for just $[X].` — 没想到 X 块就可以[Y]
- `My friends think I spent [a lot]. It was $[X].` — 朋友以为我花了几千,其实就……
- `I can handle expensive. I can't handle pretty but useless.` — 我能接受贵,接受不了好看不实用
- `If it weren't this cheap, I wouldn't use it every single day.` — 要不是便宜,我真舍不得天天用
- `Best [X] I've ever used — and it's under $[price].` — 我用过最好的 X,还不到 N 块
- `$[X], and they throw in [freebie]. How are they not losing money?` — X 块还送[赠品],我都不懂他们怎么不亏

- 西语示例(测试稿,未经数据验证):
  - `Si no te digo el precio, piensas que costó un dineral. Y no, me salió baratísimo.` — 不说价格你以为花了一大笔,其实便宜得很

- #88 "These are literally about to sell out."(储物袋,**断货必须真实**)

## 7 人群点名
直接叫出买家是谁,筛出会买的人。
**点名之后必须带一个痛点、悬念或反差,单独点名不算钩子。**(例:"养狗的?"太弱;"养狗的,你家沙发是不是也全是毛?"才算)

- `If you still haven't found your [X], watch this.` — 如果你还没找到你的 X,看看这个
  - ES: "Si todavía no tienes [XX], mira esto."
- `If you don't [X], scroll away — this is for [people who do].` — 不是 X 的请划走,这个是给 X 的
- `[Age] and under can scroll past. But if you want [X], try this.` — X 岁以下可以划走了,但要是想[X],赶紧试试
- `On a budget? Just lock this one in.` — 预算有限的,直接锁死这个
- `All the [group] keep saying [desire].` — [某人群]一直在说他们想要[X]
- `If you're good at [X], you'll know what I mean…` — 如果你很会[X],你肯定懂我在说什么(捧杀)
- `If you have a [X] at home, you need this.` — 家里有 X 的,都得备一个
- `Every guy over 30 needs one of these in his [truck].` — 男人过了 30 岁一定要有一个(人群越细越好)
- `Tell me you're a [dog mom] without telling me.` — 不直说,让我知道你是个养狗的
- `[People], stop scrolling — this [X] is for you.` — [人群]先别划,这个是给你的(**只能和人群一起用,单独的 stop scrolling 不用**)

## 8 真心推荐
建立信任:发自内心的夸、真实自用、回购。

- `Whoever invented this is a genius.` / `…deserves a raise.` / `…deserves to go straight to heaven.` — 发明这个的人是天才 / 该加薪 / 该去见上帝
  - ES: "El que inventó esto es un genio." / "A quien inventó esto, deberían subirle el sueldo." / "Quien inventó esto merece estar ya en el cielo."
- `I don't usually recommend stuff, but I can't help it with this one.` — 我一般不轻易推荐,但这个真忍不住
- `As long as they keep making it, I'll keep buying it.` — 只要不停产,我就一直买
- `I waited until my second reorder to post about this.` — 等到第二次回购我才敢来分享
- `First time was a random buy. Now I rebuy on purpose.` — 第一次是顺手买,后来是专门回购
- `Tried it once. Been reordering ever since.` — 用了一次,直接回购
- `This is the only [X] I could find that [strict requirement].` — 这是全网我唯一找到能满足我[要求]的
- `Honestly, I can't even find a flaw to nitpick.` — 这品质,我连小缺点都不好意思挑
- `I'd tell anyone to get one, whatever your budget, because [X].` — 不管你预算多少,我都会说买一个吧,因为……

- `This is basically a gift for [people].` — 这简直就是[某类人]的福利

## 9 身边人证明
别人的反应比自己夸更可信。

- `My husband says I waste money, but…` — 老公说我败家,但……

- `The one who said I buy too much stuff now uses it more than I do.` — 嫌我乱买东西的人,后来用得比我还勤
- `I got this for my mom.` — 我给妈妈买了这个
  - ES: "Se lo compré a mi mamá."
- `I learned this from a friend. Honestly thought it was a waste of money at first, but…` — 从朋友那学的,一开始觉得是浪费钱,但……

## 11 盘点清单
人们习惯接受别人总结好的东西。

- `The [N] [X] most worth buying this [season].` — 这季最值得买的 N 个 X

- `I found [3] uses for this $[X] thing — the third one is wild.` — 这个 X 块的东西我找到 3 种用法,第 3 个最离谱
- `[N] [X] that save you hours.` — N 个帮你省好几小时的 X
- `Things in my house that just make sense.` — 我家里那些"就该这样"的东西
- `Rating everything I bought on TikTok Shop this month.` — 给我这个月在 TikTok Shop 买的东西打分
- `Come test out these 2 [products] with me.` — 我们来测试这两个商品(效果对比)

## 13 借势
名人、明星、热剧、节日、事件自带流量,扯上关系就能蹭到热度。**名人说过的话、明星同款必须真实。**

- `[Celebrity] said [X], and I had to try it.` — 名人说……
- `The same [X] [celebrity] uses.` — 明星同款
- `If you're watching [hit show], you need this for [scene].` — 热剧/热点
- `You only have a week left to order for [July 4th].` — 离 7 月 4 日只剩一周(节日倒计时)
- `I know Christmas is still [six months] away, but…` — 我知道离圣诞还有半年,但……(反季节提前)
- `[Game day] is this Sunday. Here's what's in my cooler.` — 这周日比赛日(体育赛事)

## 钩子后第二句怎么接
钩子不是孤句,第二句要立刻接进第一个痛点或卖点。

- `Whoever invented this deserves a raise.` → `I've been fighting [pain] for years — you just [one action] and [result].`
- `Don't buy this.` → `…unless you want to get as obsessed as I did. I've already reordered it twice.`
- `If you [pain], don't scroll.` → `This is the [X] that finally [fixes it] — watch.`
- `I got this for my mom.` → `Now she keeps one in every room.`
- `If I'd known this existed, I wouldn't have wasted years [pain].` → `It [fixes it] in seconds — look.`
- `Everyone's pushing this — does it actually [work]?` → `I tested it. Here's what happened.`
