# CTA 库

CTA = 让观众去点商品卡的那几句。软 CTA 顺嘴一提,用"我"开头的自述;硬 CTA 说得更明确,但也是"我"的口吻,像朋友随口一提,**不用命令句**。

## 规则
1. **每条都用软 + 硬两次 CTA**,把三件套分开放:
   - **软 CTA**(放在**主卖点第一次证明之后**)= ① **行动指向**:链接在哪,用"我"开头,顺嘴一句,不停下来推销。最自然的是叠在证明画面上说,或者在对话里由对方问"在哪买的"带出。**视频短于 18 秒就不放软 CTA**,只在结尾放一次。
   - **硬 CTA**(约 80% 处到结尾,可以收口)= 用"我"的口吻说清在哪买("我就是在下面小车里买的""想要的话就在购物车里")+ ② **真实紧迫**(不造假,但必须说)+ ③ **一句收口**(回扣卖点、痛点或人群)。说的时候动作不规定:接着用商品、看着镜头说、把商品放下都行,手指向屏幕左下角只是可选的一种。
   - 两次说法不同。
2. **结尾两种都行**:硬 CTA 收口(最后一句就是真实紧迫或回扣,说完即结束,不再拖),或者硬 CTA 之后留一句反应、一个画面收尾。首尾画面呼应、循环播放靠剪辑做,不写进即梦提示词。
3. **不用命令句**:
   - ✅ 说自己怎么买的、在哪、为什么现在:`I got mine from the little cart down there.` / `It's in the cart if you want one.` / `I'd grab one while it's still on sale.` / `Yo lo saqué del carrito amarillo, ahí sigue.`
   - ❌ 叫观众去点的命令句:`Tap the yellow cart.` / `Grab yours now.` / `Pícale al carrito.`;广告按钮腔:`Shop Now.` / `Order Now.` / `BUY NOW` / `Click the link below now.` / `Don't miss this offer.`
   - `I'd grab one…`(要是我就买一个)是在说自己,可以用。
4. **台词里不报我们商品自己的价格**:具体价格、原价、折扣百分比都不说;可以说不带数字的(`it wasn't expensive`、一顿午饭钱、两杯咖啡的钱、现在在打折、没花多少)。别家的、商店的、修理厂的价格可以报真实数字("修理厂要 $150")。规格卡照样记价格,用来判断有没有折扣、能不能做机制促单。折扣、断货、限量必须真实,从商品图、详情页取(见 platform-2026.md)。
5. **收藏引导按商品分**:
   - 冲动型、低价、看完就买的 → 主 CTA 就是"去买",不加收藏,别抢走当下下单
   - 考虑型、高价、过几天才买的 → 硬 CTA 里顺带一句 "save this so you can find it later"
   - 不引导点赞
6. **轮换**:软 CTA 按下面 6 种类型轮换,同一个对话里的几条不用同一类型;硬 CTA 的说法也要错开。
   - **软、硬 CTA 不说同一个词**:两次都要说在哪买,但换个说法(软的说 `in the cart`,硬的就说 `on TikTok Shop`、`right down there` 这类),硬 CTA 的重点放在紧迫、多买、回扣卖点上。
   - **"橙色购物车"不是默认**,它只是"指位置"这一类里的一种说法。
7. **西语版**按西语样本的说法写,不从英语直译。
8. 用户说"**换 CTA**":只换 CTA 那几句,其余不动。

## 句式

### ① 行动指向
- `I'll link it down below.` / `I'll link it for you guys.`
- `It's in that orange cart.`(橙色购物车 = 平台黑话,样本高频)
- `I'll leave it right here in the shopping bag.`
- `I'll drop the link right here.`
- `Link's in the cart.`
- `If you still see the orange cart, they've still got some.` — 还能看到橙色购物车,就说明还有货
- `I'll put an orange cart down below, I got you.`
- ES: `Te dejo el enlace abajo.` / `Dejé el enlace en el carrito amarillo debajo del video.` / `Si todavía ves el carrito naranja, significa que aún hay existencias.`

### 软 CTA(6 种类型,轮换用)
| 类型 | 英语 | 西语 | 适合 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 指位置 | `I'll leave it right here.` / `It's in the cart.` | `Aquí abajito te lo dejo.` / `Está en el carrito.` | 通用,配手往下指 |
| 说在哪发现的 | `Found it on TikTok Shop, of all places.` | `Lo encontré en TikTok, ¿tú crees?` | 单人口播,带点意外感 |
| 被别人问出来 | A: `Where'd you get that?` B: `TikTok Shop.` | `—¿Dónde lo compraste? —En TikTok.` | 对话体 |
| 顺带暗示不贵 | `And no, it wasn't expensive.` | `Y no, no me costó caro.` | 价格是卖点的商品(不报数字) |
| 说已经推给别人 | `Already sent the link to my sister.` | `Ya le pasé el link a mi hermana.` | 带分享感,也顺带补上"分享"指标 |
| 你知道去哪找 | `If you want one, you know where it is.` | `Si quieres uno, ya sabes dónde está.` | 语气松弛的 |

### 硬 CTA("我"的口吻 + 紧迫,朋友随口一提)
- `It's on sale right now. I wouldn't wait.` / ES: `Está en oferta ahorita. Yo no esperaría.`
- `Honestly cost me less than lunch. It's in the cart.` / ES: `Me salió más barato que una comida. Está en el carrito.`
- `If you [人群在做的事], I'd get it before [真实时间点].` / ES: `Si [人群在做的事], yo lo agarraría antes de [真实时间点].`
- `They keep selling out, so I grabbed two while they're there.`(**断货必须真实**)

### ② 真实紧迫
- `These keep selling out for a reason.`
- `I'd get yours before your favorite colors are gone.`
- `Once people get their hands on these, they're going to sell out. I'm telling you right now.`
- `Summer's already here and these are going fast.` — 季节性紧迫
- `That sale's not going to last long, so if you want one…`
- `If the deal is still showing, it's still on.`
- `I think a bunch of them sold out, but I'll send you the link — hopefully they're still available.`
- ES: `Aprovecha que está en oferta.`
- 下面调研原句里的命令句(Pícale、Haz clic、Grab……),只借里面的说法(carrito amarillo、bolsita、selling out),口吻改成"我"。
- **不写死时间**:视频发出去会被推很多天,"今天结束""5 小时后结束"这种说法过期后会被判误导。用不写死时间的说法:`while supplies last`、`while it's still on sale`、`the price is in the cart`;只有店铺真的设了限时秒杀,且视频当天就发,才说具体时间。

### ③ 一句收口
回扣卖点:
- `This deal is just too good to pass up on.`
- `This is gonna be needed for the summer.`
- `Now I'm not wiping puddles off my counter every night.`
回扣人群:
- `If you know a [dog mom], this would be perfect.`
- `Make sure you send this to someone whose trash can looks like this.`(顺带引导分享)
- `Don't make them kids suffer.`(情感收口)

### 送礼 / 多买
用分析里的送礼、多买素材,放在硬 CTA 里。
- `It comes in a two-pack, so I put one in every room.`
- `I grabbed three sets for myself.`
- `If you know a [dad] who [X], he needs one.`
- `It'll get to you in time for [holiday].`(**到货时间必须真实**)

### 收藏引导(只用于考虑型、高价商品)
- `Save this so you can find it later.`
- `Save this — you'll thank me later.`

### 更软的写法(身份情感类,当软 CTA 用)
- `Link's down there if you want it.`
- `I'll leave it right here, mamás, check it out yourselves.`

### 不情愿式(怀疑打脸类)
不情愿的是态度,不是行动。
- `I hate to admit it, but the link's right there. I'd try it.`

### 对话体 CTA
被种草的那个人随口一句,不是主角自己推销。
- `Okay, send me the link. I'm ordering it right now.`
- `It's in the cart — I'm getting one too.`
- `TikTok? I'll send you the link.`

## 购物车怎么叫
- 英语:`yellow cart` / `yellow basket` / `orange cart` / `orange basket` / `shopping cart` 都有人说,轮换用。
- 西语:`carrito amarillo` 最常见,也说 `carrito naranja`、`bolsita amarilla`(小袋子)。

## 西语、英西混说的口吻
- 不用命令式动词(`Pícale`、`Haz clic`),用"我"的说法:`Yo lo saqué del carrito`、`Ahí está en el carrito`、`Ahí te lo dejo`。
- 名词加小词缀:`carrito`、`bolsita`,听起来不像推销。
- 称呼观众:`mi gente`、`chicos`、`my friends`。英西混说常见的是西语称呼 + 英语(`It's in the carrito amarillo, mi gente`)。

## 西语真实 CTA(调研原句,来源已核实;标"硬"的多是命令句,只借说法,不照用口吻)
- `Pícale al carrito amarillo para que no te lo pierdas.`(戳一下黄色小车,别错过)硬
- `Haz clic en el carrito amarillo para conseguir el tuyo antes de que se agoten.`(点黄色购物车,卖完之前拿到你的)硬
- `Haz clic en el carrito amarillo para elegir la tuya antes de que se agoten!`(卖完之前挑你的)硬
- `Haz clic en el carrito amarillo y estrena hoy.`(点黄色购物车,今天就用上)硬
- `Haz clic en el carrito amarillo de la izquierda para pedir la tuya.`(点左边的黄色购物车下单)硬
- `Haz clic en el carrito amarillo de aquí abajo y consigue el tuyo.`(点下面的黄色购物车)硬
- `Se los dejo para si quieren revisarlo.`(给你们放这儿了,想看看的话)软
- `Pondré el enlace abajo por si quieres echarle un vistazo.`(链接放下面了,想看一眼的话)软
- `Este producto es de TikTok Shop. Si te interesa puedes hacer clic en el enlace abajo.`(TikTok Shop 的东西,感兴趣点下面链接)软
- `Ahorita tienen un mega descuento con el cupón especial. Pícale al carrito amarillo.`(现在有优惠券大折扣,戳黄色小车)硬,**折扣必须真实**
