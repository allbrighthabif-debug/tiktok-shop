# 说法库:形容词、风格、颜色、面料、版型、痛点、身材、上身效果、场景词

第 2 步写台词时,按这件衣服的类别挑。来源是抖音服装赛道的常用词,换成了美区用户自己会说的说法。西语按同样的思路现写,不从英语直译。

## 一、怎么用
- **只学说法,不照抄原句**;同一个窗口里任何一句不重复。
- **词放进句子里**,描述具体的东西给她的感觉和画面,一句一两个;不单独喊一个形容词,不一句里堆三四个(`02-script.md` 第六节)。
- **结果前置**:先说穿上的结果(在哪穿、穿着什么感觉),再带出是哪个设计做到的;第九节的句子都是这个写法。
- 风格词、chic、effortless 带上具体对象说("the drape is so effortless"),不单独当一句夸。
- 面料、弹力、厚薄、透不透、不起球、不缩水、不掉色,按规格卡和详情页说,不编。
- 身材词只说她自己的(按角色卡);说自己的肉可以,笑着说;不贬低别人的身材。
- 可以提品牌,当场景、当比较("my Target-run outfit""softer than my Lululemon");不说"XX 的平替 / dupe",不说在别家买的。
- 季节按实际发布日期,不照抄句子里的季节。

## 二、形容词
- 面料手感:buttery soft, thick, cozy, breathable, lightweight, stretchy, silky, crisp / suavecito, fresquito, calientito, bien suave
- 上身:flattering(带对象:so flattering on the hips), hugs in all the right places, not clingy, drapey, roomy, snatched / te queda pintado, cómodo, no aprieta, se acomoda solito
- 看着:expensive-looking / se ve carísimo, bien arreglada
- 颜色:deep, buttery / bien intenso
- **比喻、动词和反差句**(留想象空间用;只学说法,按这件衣服、她的生活和这条的场景现想,不用最俗的):
  - 软(让人想到陷进去、被抱住):like wearing a cloud, feels like a hug, softer than [她生活里软的东西] / como una nube, suavecito como peluche
  - 轻(想到像没穿):feels like nothing, light as air / ni se siente
  - 暖(想到冷天被裹住):like a blanket I can wear out, my built-in heater / como cobijita
  - 合身(想到量身做的):fits like a second skin, made for my hips / me queda como hecho a la medida
  - 垂、飘(想到走起来):swishes when I walk, moves with me / se mueve bonito
  - 挺括(想到被收拾好):holds its shape all day, makes me stand up straighter
  - 动词:hugs, drapes, skims, swishes, melts, cinches
  - 反差句:穿着像 A,看着像 B(睡衣和出门装、卫衣和很贵的衣服)
  - 夸张的真实反应:never taking this off, would literally sleep in this

## 三、风格
这一节的词用来定风格方向、写标签,台词里不单独说;vibe、aesthetic、elevated、polished 这类要说就带上具体的东西(`02-script.md` 第六节)。
- 松弛、慵懒、不费力时髦:effortless, "zero effort, full outfit", lazy-girl outfit, cozy but cute / sin esfuerzo, cómoda pero linda
- 多巴胺、活力:dopamine dressing, instant mood boost, fun color, a head-turner / te alegra el día
- 在逃公主:princess energy / de princesa
- 复古、90s、千禧、美式复古:90s vibes, Y2K, vintage-inspired, very 70s, Americana / vibra noventera, estilo vintage
- 大地色、有层次:earth tones, so good layered
- 休闲百搭:goes with everything, wardrobe staple, wear it on repeat, elevated basics / combina con todo
- 老钱风、富婆感:old money, rich-girl energy, "no logos, just quality", looks expensive / se ve de dinero(quiet luxury 这个词美区创作者开始说过时了,少单独用)
- 知识分子、知性:dark academia(棕色、格子款), clean and minimal, librarian chic
- 法式:French girl style, very Parisian / muy francesa
- 工装:utility, workwear|甜酷:sweet but edgy|辣妹:baddie, Y2K
- 学院、英伦:preppy, dark academia|通勤、干练:business casual, polished, office-ready / lista para la oficina
- 极简、盐系、性冷淡:minimalist, clean girl, neutrals|森系:cottagecore
- 御姐、轻熟:elevated, office siren
- 芭蕾、少女、甜美:balletcore, coquette, soft and feminine, dainty, girly / coqueta, femenina
- ins 风、氛围感:very Pinterest, aesthetic, a whole vibe
- 混搭、反差:"it shouldn't work but it does", unexpected combo
- 户外、山系、机能:gorpcore, granola girl, "trail to brunch", athleisure|科技感:techwear, futuristic
- 新中式:说细节本身(mandarin collar, frog buttons, so graceful),不打文化牌
- 韩系、日系:不按国家叫,说具体的样子(clean and soft、relaxed and layered)
- 不用:纯欲、清冷破碎感、AI 女友穿搭(我们本身是 AI 视频)、港风、国潮、禅意、卷王、战神、科技姬

## 四、颜色
- 美拉德、拿铁焦糖、巧克力:mocha, caramel, camel, cinnamon, chocolate brown
- 薄荷:mint, pistachio, sage|高级灰:charcoal, heather gray, "the chicest gray"|经典黑:"black goes with everything"
- 配色高级、气场强:the color combo is so chic, power color
- 显白:说颜色衬她,"this color makes my skin glow" / "este color me ilumina la cara";不衬就说 "washes me out"
- 黄皮、黑黄皮:warm undertones, olive skin(美区流行色彩季型分析 color analysis),说这个颜色适合什么底色

## 五、面料、做工、细节
- 垂坠:the drape, flows when I walk, swishy / cae bonito|挺括有型:structured, holds its shape, crisp
- 空气感:light as air, airy, "feels like nothing" / ni se siente
- 亲肤顺滑:buttery soft, silky, smooth on my skin|不扎:not itchy, not scratchy at all(针织最管用)/ no pica
- 透气不闷:breathable, "I don't get sweaty in it"(要有依据)|不臃肿:not bulky, doesn't bulk me up
- 对格对条:"the plaid lines up at the seams"|锁边、压褶:clean seams, no loose threads, crisp pleats
- 刺绣、提花、肌理:delicate embroidery, the texture up close, jacquard
- 五金、纽扣:the hardware, gold buttons
- 简约不单调:simple but not boring, not basic
- 细节控:for my detail girlies, "the details!!", "whoever designed this gets it"
- 不廉价:doesn't look cheap at all, looks way more expensive(不说价格)
- 印花:说图案本身(什么图案、在哪);不掉色、不开裂只按详情页说

## 六、版型和品类名
- 版型:A-line(flares out, twirl-worthy)、straight cut / relaxed fit、boxy(最近流行,是夸的说法)、high-waisted(hits right at my waist, legs for days)、tailored(the cut is so good)
- 上衣:drop shoulder(落肩)、structured shoulder(正肩)、cropped、oversized、hits at the hip、French tuck(塞衣角)、mock neck(小立领)、collared shirt、cardigan
- 外套:blazer、oversized blazer、cropped jacket、longline coat、varsity jacket(棒球服)、tweed jacket(小香风)、trench
- 裤子:straight-leg、wide-leg、cigarette pants(烟管裤)、joggers(束脚裤)、kick flare(微喇)、cargo pants
- 裙子:A-line、skater skirt(伞裙)、pleated skirt、column skirt(直筒裙)、cinched-waist dress、midi(到膝下)
- 领口、袖子、裤裙的效果说法见 `fit-body.md` 第二节。

## 七、痛点(说得轻、好笑)
- 起球、掉色、缩水、变形:pills after one wash, fades, bleeds in the wash, shrinks, loses its shape / se hace bolitas, se despinta, se encoge
- 透:see-through, "can you see my underwear?" / se transparenta
- 皱、静电、粘毛、勾丝:wrinkles the second I sit, static cling, lint magnet, snags on everything
- 扎、闷:itchy, scratchy, "I'm sweating in this"
- 勒、往上跑:waistband digs in, leaves marks, rides up, waist gap, thigh chafe / me aprieta, se sube
- 色差、尺码不准、廉价:"looks nothing like the photo", sizing all over the place, looks cheap
- 网红款踩雷:"TikTok made me buy it… and it flopped"
- 衣柜爆炸、不会搭:"closet full, nothing to wear", "cute on the hanger, no idea what to wear it with"
- 长度尴尬、压个子:too short to sit down in, "it swallows me", drowning in fabric
- 撑不起、臃肿、没垂感:shoulder seams hang off, bulky, stiff
- 身材:说成她自己的担心,这件照顾到了("I was worried about my arms"、my tummy、love handles、hip dips、the bra bulge)
- 说"这件解决了"要有依据(不起球、不缩水按详情页)。

## 八、身材
- 自称(只说角色卡上她自己的):petite, "5'2 girls", tall girls, midsize, plus-size(尺码表有大码才说), pear-shaped, apple shape, hourglass, athletic build, broad shoulders, long torso, short legs, "if you've got hips", thick thighs, busty, small chest
- 遮肉、显瘦:hides everything I don't want seen, smooths it all out, so slimming, snatched, "I look a whole size smaller", hides my tummy, no muffin top, tummy control, "hides my arms", smooths the bra bulge, covers my thighs, "bowlegged girls, this is your sign" / me hace ver más delgada, disimula la pancita
- 谁都能穿:looks good on literally everyone, flattering on every body, works on every body type, "my mom and my niece both want one" / le queda a todas
- 均码:one size fits most / talla única
- 塑身衣不说减重、减寸、燃脂。

## 九、上身效果句(结果前置)
- 舒服:
  - "I've been at my desk since eight and forgot I had it on— the waistband just doesn't dig in."
  - "Chased my toddler around the park all morning— nothing rode up, nothing pinched."
  - "Had a whole pasta lunch and the waistband didn't budge."
  - "Soft enough to nap in, cute enough for dinner."
  - ES "Llevo todo el día sentada y ni me acuerdo que lo traigo puesto."
- 合身、遮肉:
  - "I didn't suck in once— it skims, it doesn't cling."
  - "Midsize girls, it's roomy where I need it and I still have a waist."
- 比例:
  - "I'm 5'2 and it doesn't swallow me— the waist hits right where it should."
  - "Legs for days, and I'm in flats."
  - "The flare kicks out right at the ankle, so even in sneakers my legs go on forever."
- 气质、氛围:
  - "He hasn't stopped staring since I walked out— it's the way the waist cinches."(男友拍)
  - "Office-appropriate but not boring— the drape makes it look so expensive."
  - "Threw the blazer over a tee and suddenly I look like I run the meeting."
- 百搭省心:
  - "Pulled it on, grabbed my keys, done— and I still look like I tried."
  - "Desk at nine, drinks at six, didn't change a thing."
  - "Sneakers today, heels Friday— works both ways."
  - "French tuck it for work, leave it out on weekends."
  - ES "Con tenis o con tacones, queda igual de bonito."
- 细节、面料:
  - "It swings every time I take a step— I keep spinning on purpose."
  - "The wide legs swish when I walk— I feel like I'm floating."
  - "Not itchy at all, and it's right on my skin."
- 身份、身边的人:"Teachers, this is your Monday outfit." / "My mom already asked to borrow it."(剧情)
- 不用:闭眼冲、直接冲、姐妹必入、天花板这类口号(和 `cta.md` 禁用清单一类)。
